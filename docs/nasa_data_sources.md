# NASA & Partner Earth Observation Data Sources

This document records the exact provenance, GEE Collection IDs, spatial and temporal resolutions, extracted scientific variables, transformations, and limitations for all Earth observation datasets integrated into **AgriShift**.

## 2. Complementary Regional & Agronomic Data Sources

- **Bangladesh Soil Resource Development Institute (SRDI) & BARC Fertilizer Recommendation Guide (2024):** Provides Agro-Ecological Zone (**AEZ-13, AEZ-11, AEZ-14**) soil electrical conductivity (`ECe` in `dS/m`) baselines, pH ranges, and nutrient recommendations.
- **BRRI, BARI, and BINA Varietal Guidelines:** Curated in [`agronomy/Crop Database/AgriShift_Khulna_Crop_Database.xlsx`](../agronomy/Crop%20Database/AgriShift_Khulna_Crop_Database.xlsx), defining stage-specific salinity thresholds (`ECe`), water demand, and growth durations for coastal rice, oilseed, and pulse varieties.
- **MIT Eltahir Group Bangladesh Soil Salinity Projections:** Referenced in `/salinity-prediction` (`https://www.mit.edu/~eltahirgroup/DST_SoilSalinity/`) for regional historical maps (2000–2016) and SSP5-8.5 future projections (2030, 2050).
