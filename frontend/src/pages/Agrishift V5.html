What you are looking at

Measured

Measured data

Your SRDI soil card values (EC, pH, N, P, K) or a live probe reading. The app does not estimate these.

Derived

Derived values

Effective EC, net nutrient dose and root moisture are calculated from your inputs and the scenario. The formulas are in the Evidence panel.

Assumed

Scenario assumptions

Rainfall shift, added salinity, water availability and the 2025 to 2050 timeline are what-if settings you control. MIT projections and NASA charts are context.

Illustrative

Illustrative thresholds

Crop scores come from a transparent rule-based engine, not an AI model. Tolerances, nutrient factors and the legume credit are illustrative, pending expert validation.

    Data sources

    MIT DST soil salinity model

    ML-projected peak dry-season (Mar–Apr) ECe, SSP5-8.5, 5-year steps to 2050 (Eltahir group, MIT). Drawn as an image overlay when the map library is active; values read from it are approximate.

    NASA SMAP

    Satellite soil-moisture estimates. Not a salinity sensor.

    NASA GPM

    Satellite-based precipitation estimates.

    NASA POWER

    Meteorological and solar time series derived from satellite and model data.

    NASA MODIS

    Vegetation greenness (NDVI). Crop-condition context, not a salinity measurement.

    Place values

    Marker values for places are illustrative fixtures adjusted by the timeline. In this demo the NASA series are cached, not live.
